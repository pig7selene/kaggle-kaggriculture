# Rank 3 strategy dossier — OceanMix

Current submission `55926618`; public score 2862.3; 30 current-version episodes.

## Economic thesis

This economy uses a repeatable mixed-animal opening, a day-6 first expansion and a melon-funded day-11 second expansion. Several episodes share an exact action route, but later crop and market phases still branch; it is best described as phase-adaptive rather than fixed.

## Discovered phases

| Phase | Objective | Capital and assets | Market exposure / transition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening (0–150) | Build feed plus first premium crop and service initial livestock | Initial seeds, animals and early hands; preserves enough liquidity for land | Early wheat/fertilizer/animal-product sales; transitions when first land is affordable |
| First reinvestment (150–265) | Turn first animal/crop cash into 50-tile capacity | First land, additional animals, larger daily labor | Milk/wool/fertilizer realization funds expansion; transitions on second-land cash |
| Second reinvestment (265–336) | Populate three quadrants without starving animal service | Second land, wheat/melon/strawberry cohorts, hand ramp | First large melon/premium wave recycles into seeds and labor |
| Scaled midgame (336–552) | Maintain simultaneous crop cohorts and indefinite animal production | High productive-tile count; recurring feed/care/harvest | Diversified selling reduces dependence on any one glut |
| Late production (552–672) | Finish profitable recurring and short-cycle crops | New planting narrows; existing livestock continues | Premium products are staggered rather than held for the whole season |
| Liquidation (672–719) | Convert all remaining harvestable value to bank | Planting stops, harvesting/shed logistics dominate | Last sales cluster near step 718; terminal inventory is audited |
