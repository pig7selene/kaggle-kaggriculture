# Top-10 public strategy research and controlled adjustment

Date: 2026-09-02  
Frozen local best: `agents/top50_distilled/top50_observable_portfolio.py`  
No changes were made to `submission/main.py`.

## 1. Evidence used

The existing downloaded V3 Top-10 corpus was used without re-downloading:

- 10 selected public submission versions (ranks 1–10)
- 126 valid unique replay IDs
- 82 deduplicated development appearances used for route mining
- 3 structural families
- zero development financial-reconstruction mismatches

The compact reproducible synthesis is generated by
`analyze_top10_meta.py`; machine-readable output is
`experiments/top10_meta_summary.json` and the route table is in
`experiments/top10_meta_summary.md`.

## 2. Shared high-rating patterns

| pattern | Top-10 evidence | interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| three-quadrant economy | all 10 routes | the extra capacity is part of the capital plan, not a late luxury |
| first land | steps 144–160 (day 6), mode 160 | early land is funded by aggressive opening cash cycling |
| second land | steps 217–264 (days 9–11), mode 240 | the second deed is purchased only after the first crop/animal cash wave |
| melon wave | 10/10 | early premium crop funds expansion |
| strawberry wave | 10/10 | the midgame is premium-crop heavy, not a static melon monoculture |
| mixed cows + sheep | 10/10 in the extracted route states | wool and milk provide a cash bridge while crops mature |
| 12–14 hands | all 10 reach a high labor ceiling | labor scales with the three-quadrant workload |
| terminal selling | last sale mode step 718 | valuable inventory is cleared at the end |

The dominant fixed family is JALKARNA-like (ranks 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10):
160/240 land timing, eight cows/four sheep, 14 hands, and a stable
melon→strawberry→wheat schedule.  Rank 1 and rank 2 are genuinely adaptive:
their early field is stable, but worker/crop/market actions diverge materially
from the first expansion onward.  Their raw routes are not safe generic
parents: earlier local tests observed animal-loss cascades for rank 1 and
negative paired transfer for rank 2.

## 3. Difference versus our current best

The current Top-50 portfolio already captures the broad consensus through
three coherent safety-repaired parents.  Its parents use 150/265 land timing,
12 hands, and 7/8/9 cows with 4/6/5 sheep.  The Top-10 evidence therefore
does not justify blindly changing land, labor, or animal counts.  The one
remaining structural family not represented as a current parent is the stable
JALKARNA-like 160/240 route.  Because route-bank compatibility collapses after
the opening, it must be selected once at step 1; it cannot be spliced in the
midgame.

## 4. Controlled adjustment

Two small probes were made:

1. `top10_jalkarna_parent_v1.py` attempted to add the raw JALKARNA route when
   the opponent exposed its low-bank/four-hand opening.  A direct four-game
   diagnostic against that family ended with roughly 10–14k own money versus
   120–170k for the opponent and repeated livestock losses.  It was discarded
   as unsafe; it is retained only as a research artifact.
2. `top10_lowbank_hanserong_v1.py` kept the proven parent set and routed that
   same signature to the safety-repaired Hanserong (7-cow/6-sheep) parent.
   This tests Top-10 livestock diversity without importing an unsafe route.

The second probe used fresh seeds 57900–57901, both seats, fixed and natural
shop schedules, the ten Top-10 routes, and seven hard local opponents: 272
complete 720-turn games.  All rows had zero runtime and semantic failures.

The own-money increase is not a competitive improvement: it is accompanied by
lower opponent-relative advantage, a much worse lower tail, and 64 purchased
animals missing from the final state.  The change was therefore rejected.

## 5. Conclusion and next direction

Top-10 mining confirms the capital skeleton but does not support a safe static
parent change.  The adaptive leaders' value is state-conditioned continuation
behavior, not a different universal opening.  The current Top-50 portfolio
remains the strongest robust local strategy.

The highest-value next step is a state-compatible residual executor that owns
crop cohorts, animal service, inventory, and market capacity together, then
learns from the rank-1/rank-2 adaptive branches.  A late route splice or a
single low-bank livestock switch is not reliable evidence and should not be
promoted.
